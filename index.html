About ABBAS AGRO FARM

Committed to sustainable agriculture and community development

Our Story

Located in the fertile lands of Khashkarara Union, Chuadanga, in the Khulna Division of Bangladesh, ABBAS AGRO FARM has been dedicated to sustainable organic farming practices since its establishment. We believe in nurturing the land while providing high-quality organic products to our community and beyond.
